The 4 Stages of a US Election

  1. 1

    Registration

    Register before your state's deadline at vote.gov.

    • Update if you've moved
  2. 2

    Verification

    Confirm registration at vote.org and find your polling place.

  3. 3

    Voting Day

    Polls typically open 6 AM – 8 PM. Bring a valid photo ID.

    • If in line at closing — you can still vote
    • Absentee/early voting available in most states
  4. 4

    Results

    Results are announced election night; final certification takes days to weeks.

How to Vote in the UK

1. Register

Register at gov.uk/register-to-vote at least 12 working days before election.

  • Must be 18+ on election day
  • UK, Irish, or qualifying Commonwealth citizen

2. Photo ID

Since 2023, you must bring photo ID to vote (passport, driving licence, etc.).

  • Check accepted IDs at electoralcommission.org.uk

3. Voting Day

Polls open 7 AM – 10 PM. Mark an X next to your chosen candidate.

  • First Past The Post system — highest vote-count wins

4. Results

Counting starts after polls close. Results typically by early next morning.

  • electoralcommission.org.uk

How to Vote in Australia

1. Enrol

Enrol at aec.gov.au/Enrolment. Automatic for citizens 18+.

  • Must be Australian citizen
  • Voting is compulsory — fines apply

2. No Photo ID Needed

Your name and address confirm your enrolment at the polling place.

3. Voting Day

Polls open 8 AM – 6 PM. Number ALL candidates in order of preference.

  • Preferential voting — rank candidates 1, 2, 3…
  • Postal and early voting available

4. Results

Preference counting takes time — final result may take days.

  • aec.gov.au

How to Vote in Canada

1. Register

Register at ereg.elections.ca or on election day at your polling station.

  • Must be 18+ Canadian citizen

2. ID

Bring one piece of ID with name and address, OR two pieces with name.

3. Voting Day

Polls open 9:30 AM – 9:30 PM local time. Mark an X next to your chosen candidate.

  • First Past The Post system
  • Advanced polls open 4 days before election day

4. Results

Results are announced election night as polls close across time zones.

  • elections.ca
